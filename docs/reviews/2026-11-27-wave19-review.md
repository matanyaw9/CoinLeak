## The keys (part A)

- **One Tab stop.**
  - Every name in the Sankey takes its `tabIndex` from `useRoving` (`flow-sankey.tsx`), so exactly one is 0.
  - The roving keys are `kind:categoryId`. In both the top flow and a group's flow, no two nodes share one, because each child is either refund or spending, never both.
  - Up, Down, Home and End move. Tab leaves the chart.
- **Recharts' own focus is off.** In recharts 3.10, `accessibilityLayer` (on by default) gives the `<svg>` a `tabIndex` of 0 and the role "application". `accessibilityLayer={false}` removes both. No other focusable element is left in the drawing.
- **Enter does what a click does** (`pickOf`, tested):
  - It opens one level down or opens Transactions.
  - A held-down Enter is ignored (`e.repeat`).
  - On a name that does nothing (the hub, an income), Enter does nothing, which is right.
- **Tooltip on focus.** Only keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) moves the tooltip to the bar. A mouse click keeps the mouse's own tooltip.
- **Focus after Enter on a name** goes to the first name on the right of the new level:
  - Each level is keyed (`GroupBody key`), so it mounts fresh and `takeFocus` runs once.
  - While a level that is not cached yet loads, focus is on the page for a moment, then lands. That is acceptable.
- **The `?` list** has the "charts" line.
